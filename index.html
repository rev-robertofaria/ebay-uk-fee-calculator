About

eBay UK Fee Calculator estimates what eBay.co.uk charges UK-registered sellers and what is left after your own costs. It is free, open source (MIT) and runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type leaves your device.

Please note. This calculator is the result of careful work transcribing and testing eBay’s published fee rules, but it is not guaranteed to be free of errors. Treat the figures as a close estimate of what you will pay, and always confirm against eBay.co.uk and your own Seller Hub order details before setting prices.

Not affiliated with eBay. eBay is a trademark of eBay Inc. The rates here are transcribed from eBay's public help pages (see Rates for the verification date). Fees change; your Seller Hub order details are always the final word.

How it calculates

  • Business: final value fee % by category on item + postage, tiered rates per item, per-order fee (£0.30 / £0.40, reduced 10p where eBay says so), 0.35% regulatory operating fee, international fee by the buyer's delivery region, optional surcharges and Top Rated discount, then 20% VAT on fees unless you are VAT registered.
  • Private (UK): no final value fee. The Buyer Protection fee (£0.10 + 7% to £20, 4% to £300, 2% to £4,000) is paid by the buyer on top of your price — the calculator shows the price the buyer sees. 3% international fee if posting abroad. VAT is added to Promoted Listings fees.
  • eBay Live: commission (6%, or 4% for coins, bullion and sneakers, capped at £100 per item) + 2.42% payment processing + per-order fee.
  • Assumption flagged for confirmation: the £10 per-order threshold is applied to the order total including postage.

Out of scope

  • Motors (cars, motorcycles, vehicles).
  • Listings on other eBay sites (eBay.de, eBay.com…): that site's fee card applies, plus the GBP currency conversion charge.
  • Fee credits on refunds and cancellations; postage label prices.

Install & offline

Chrome / Edge: use the Install button. iPhone / iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen. After the first visit the calculator works without internet.

Privacy

No analytics, no cookies, no accounts. Your last scenario is kept in your browser's local storage only.
